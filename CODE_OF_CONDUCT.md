# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to creating a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful community for all contributors and users of agentspend, regardless of background, identity, experience, or perspective.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy toward other community members
- Crediting contributors appropriately

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, discrimination, or hate speech
- Personal attacks or trolling
- Publishing private information without consent
- Sexually explicit or violent content
- Deliberate disruption of community spaces
- Spam or low-effort contributions

## Enforcement

Community members who violate this code of conduct may be subject to:

- A warning from maintainers
- Temporary or permanent ban from community spaces
- Removal of contributions

## Reporting

If you witness or experience a violation of this code of conduct, please report it to **manoj.mallick079@gmail.com** with:

- Description of the incident
- Who was involved
